          HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (HMS) - SIMPLE CLIENT GUIDE

A quick, plain-language guide explaining what this software does and how it 
helps your hospital or clinic run smoothly.

1. WHAT IS THIS SOFTWARE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is an all-in-one digital system for clinics and hospitals. It replaces paper 
files, registers, and spreadsheets with a single, easy-to-use software that runs 
on computers and tablets.

Everything connects in real time: from the front desk to the doctor's room, 
inpatient beds, pharmacy, lab, and finance office.


2. WHO IS IT FOR?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Private Medical Clinics & Polyclinics
* Hospitals (Small, Medium, and Large)
* Diagnostic Labs & Pathology Centers
* Hospital & Retail Pharmacies
* Healthcare Chains with Multiple Branches


3. MAIN FEATURES AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] RECEPTION & FRONT DESK
* Instant Patient Search: Find any returning patient in seconds by name, phone, 
  or CNIC/ID number.
* 1-Click Registration: Register new patients quickly without long paperwork.
* Token & Queue System: Issue printed tokens for walk-ins, OPD consultations, 
  and appointments.
* Emergency Fast-Track: Instantly register critical patients with zero delays.

[2] DOCTOR DASHBOARD & CLINICAL WORKSPACE
* Live Waiting List: Doctors see waiting patients in real time and call them in.
* Fast Digital Prescriptions: Pick medicines from the pharmacy list, set dosage 
  (e.g., 1 tablet after meals), and print a neat branded prescription.
* Medical Notes & Vitals: Record Blood Pressure, Pulse, Sugar, complaints, and 
  diagnoses.
* Order Tests & Referrals: Order lab tests or refer to another doctor with one click.

[3] PATIENT MEDICAL HISTORY (EMR)
* Lifetime Medical Record: Every patient has a permanent digital file.
* Full History in 1 Place: View all past doctor visits, prescriptions, test 
  results, and bills on one clean timeline.
* Allergy Alerts: Clear warnings for drug allergies and high-risk health conditions.

[4] ROOM & BED MANAGEMENT (INPATIENT / IPD)
* Visual Live Bed Map: Color-coded map showing which beds are Free (Green), 
  Occupied (Red), or Cleaning (Blue).
* Easy Admissions: Admit a patient to a General Ward, Private Room, or ICU in seconds.
* 1-Click Discharge: Automatically totals room charges, medicines, and doctor fees, 
  then generates an official Discharge Summary.

[5] PHARMACY & MEDICINE STOCK
* Fast Counter Sales: Quick barcode/search billing for walk-in customers.
* Prescription Dispensing: Fulfill doctor prescriptions with a single click.
* Expiry & Low-Stock Alerts: Warns you before medicines expire or run out of stock.

[6] LABORATORY & DIAGNOSTICS
* Test Catalog: Pre-configured test prices and normal health ranges.
* Step-by-Step Tracking: Sample collected -> Testing -> Pathologist Verified.
* Smart Results: Highlights abnormal values in bold so they are never missed.
* Professional PDF Reports: Ready to print or hand over to patients.

[7] BILLING, EXPENSES & FINANCE
* Combined Invoices: Combines doctor fees, bed charges, pharmacy, and lab into 
  one clear bill.
* Multiple Payment Methods: Accept Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, or Insurance.
* Expense Tracker: Record hospital expenses (rent, utilities, supplies, maintenance).
* Unpaid Dues Tracking: Instantly see who owes money and track pending payments.
* Profit & Loss Reports: Know your exact daily, weekly, and monthly earnings.

[8] STAFF, ATTENDANCE & 1-CLICK PAYROLL
* Staff Directory: Contact details, roles, shifts, and salaries for all employees.
* Daily Attendance: Tracks check-in, check-out, and late arrivals automatically.
* Leave Management: Simple system for staff leave requests and approvals.
* 1-Click Monthly Payroll: Automatically calculates salaries, late deductions, 
  and bonuses, and prints ready-made payslips.

[9] HOSPITAL OWNER DASHBOARD
* Live Daily Overview: See today's earnings, total patients, occupied beds, and 
  doctor workload on one screen.
* Revenue Charts: Visual graphs showing which departments make the most income.
* Multi-Branch Switcher: If you have 2 or more clinic locations, switch between 
  them or view total combined revenue with one click.

[10] CUSTOM BRANDING & LOGO
* Your Own Hospital Branding: Displays your hospital logo, contact info, and 
  colors on all printed receipts, prescriptions, and reports.


4. WHY YOUR HOSPITAL WILL BENEFIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Faster Service: Cuts patient wait times at reception and pharmacy by over 50%.
2. No Lost Money: Every medicine, test, and bed day is automatically billed.
3. 100% Paperless: Clean, organized, and no risk of lost physical files.
4. Total Owner Control: Know your hospital's financial health anytime, anywhere.
5. Simple to Learn: Staff can learn and start using it in less than 30 minutes.
