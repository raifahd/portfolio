Zaman Arts E-Commerce App
A feature-rich Flutter e-commerce application for browsing and purchasing arts and crafts products with integrated video content, real-time notifications, and seamless checkout experience.

Overview
The Zaman Arts app is a modern mobile e-commerce platform that combines product discovery, video content, and smooth shopping functionality. Users can browse products, watch detailed videos, manage a shopping cart, place orders, and track their purchases—all with a polished, intuitive interface.

Key Features
Product Discovery
Browse products with grid and video layouts
Search functionality for finding specific items
Curated collections for organized browsing
Product filtering and sorting capabilities
Like and comment on products
Cached product images for better performance
Video Content
Integrated video player for product demonstrations
Video preloading for smooth playback
Like and comment interactions on videos
Liked videos tracking in user profile
Chewie video player with advanced controls
Shopping Experience
Add products to cart
Manage cart quantities and items
Checkout with multiple payment options
Deep linking support for direct product access
Order tracking and history
Return/refund request management
User Management
Google Sign-In integration
Email/password authentication
User profile management
Address management for shipping
Order history and returns tracking
Account preferences
Notifications
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) integration
Push notifications for orders and updates
Local notifications support
Real-time notification handling
Additional Features
Share products and content
Dark mode support with custom theme
Edge-to-edge display optimization
Offline cache support via SharedPreferences
Firebase integration for backend services
